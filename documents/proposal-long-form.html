What this proposal commits to

Envision Lab proposes a two-day, in-person simulation programme for 18–24 senior ward leaders. The programme rehearses the core decisions of intrinsic-capacity care under real ward constraints. Participants leave with a reusable diagnostic protocol, a referral pathway they have authored together, and calibrated confidence in their use of the WHO ICOPE screen.

What you can expect

  • Six learning outcomes, each tied to an action verb and a measurable artefact.
  • One hero simulation across both days, with three escalating rounds.
  • A senior-only delivery team. The designers stay on the work end-to-end.
  • A briefing report after the cohort, summarising decisions, patterns, and recommendations to your leadership.
