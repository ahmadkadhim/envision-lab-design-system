About Envision Lab

Dubai-based professional and management development institute. Boutique by design.

Envision Lab is a Dubai-based professional and management development institute specialising in applied, scenario-based learning for government, semi-government, and corporate clients across the UAE and the wider region. Programmes are developed in-house by our learning design team, grounded in adult learning principles and behavioural science, and evaluated against ISO 29993 learning service standards.
