Roadmap

Five milestones across twelve weeks. Design and delivery sit with the same senior team end to end.

Week 1
Discovery
Ward shadowing, stakeholder interviews, decision-log review.
Week 3
Co-design
Scenarios drafted with two senior nurses; rubric calibrated.
Weeks 5–6
Delivery
Two cohorts, two days each. Decisions captured against rubric.
Week 8
Embed
Each leader runs their 30-day change. Weekly check-in held.
Week 12
Impact
Re-test on rubric. Findings written into ward standard.
