Learning Outcomes

Each outcome is paired with a measurement instrument applied pre, in-session, and at 30 days.

01
Diagnose intrinsic-capacity decline using the WHO ICOPE screen.
Measured by inter-rater reliability of two facilitators against a third-party benchmark.
02
Decide the referral pathway under named constraints.
Measured by appropriateness against ward-level escalation rules in the live simulation.
03
Deliver an SBAR handover to an on-call medic.
Measured by completeness against a six-item rubric, scored by the receiving facilitator.
04
Defend the decision against a structured debrief.
Measured by how the leader names the trade-off they made — and what would have changed it.
